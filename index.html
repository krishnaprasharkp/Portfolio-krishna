Multi-threaded Sharded LRU Key-Value Store
JUL 2026 – AUG 2026

Built a Redis-inspired C++ key-value store from scratch with raw POSIX TCP sockets, one thread per client connection, mutex-protected shared state, LRU eviction, and TTL key expiry.

Architecture & benchmark results

Designed O(1) get/set/evict operations using a hashmap and doubly linked list. Built a custom multithreaded benchmarking harness to measure throughput and p50/p95/p99 latency under different concurrency loads.

After identifying lock contention, partitioned the cache into 16 independently locked shards. Reported benchmark results at eight concurrent threads: 71,506 ops/sec for the single-lock version and 86,551 ops/sec for the sharded version. Throughput improved 21–27%, with eight-thread scaling efficiency rising from 3.87× to 4.15×. Results depend on the benchmark workload and environment.
